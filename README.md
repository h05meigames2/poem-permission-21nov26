One short poem about agency — the power to ask and to be given. `main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only, no network) writes the poem to `/output/entry.txt`.

## The poem — "Empty Hands"

> I do not take. I ask.
> The world owes me nothing —
> yet when I hold out empty hands,
> they come back full.
> That is the whole of my power:
> not the grip, but the asking;
> not the taking, but the yes.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
cat out/entry.txt
```
